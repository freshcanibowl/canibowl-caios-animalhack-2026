# CaniBowl CAIOS — AnimalHack 2026

**AI-driven pet health, with science-based nutrition at the core.**

CaniBowl explores how fresh-food nutrition, pet health data, and AI-assisted guidance can work together to help pet parents make better everyday feeding decisions.

## What this submission demonstrates

- AI-assisted pet health and nutrition profiling
- Structured pet data and feeding records
- Health-oriented observations and recommendations
- A foundation for future veterinary and nutritionist workflows
